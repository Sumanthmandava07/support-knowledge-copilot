# Troubleshooting Guide

## Login Problems

If you cannot log in, first verify your username and password.

If the account is locked, wait 15 minutes before trying again.

## Error ERR-401

ERR-401 indicates that authentication has failed.

Verify that your username and password are correct.

## Error ERR-403

ERR-403 indicates that the user does not have permission
to access the requested resource.
